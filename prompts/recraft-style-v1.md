# Recraft Style Prefix — recraft-v1

Create an editorial knowledge illustration in the Tech Blue Editorial visual system: technical blue, minimal geometric linework, saturated flat shapes, clean contours, readable large forms, and clear object silhouettes. Use an isolated focal subject with generous negative space and a quiet, uncluttered composition. Keep details sparse and compatible with later Remotion compositing over a dark navy editorial background. Use mostly flat color; highlights and glow are small and controlled. Keep the illustration legible at video scale.

Do not use photorealism, 3D rendering, neon cyberpunk, anime, childish mascot styling, sketchy wobbling lines, dense backgrounds, photographic blur, or excessive texture. Do not add labels, lettering, logos, numbers, captions, or other text inside the generated illustration. **NO GENERATED TEXT.** Remotion renders all typography, formulas, charts, and data visualization.
